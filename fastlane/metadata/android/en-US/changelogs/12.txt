Safer saving, callouts and collapsible sections.

- GitHub-style callouts (NOTE, TIP, WARNING…) and collapsible sections.
- Files in older text formats are never overwritten; Save offers a copy instead.
- Opening a file never replaces unsaved edits without asking, and text typed during a save is kept.
- Maths follows your reading size; tall formulas are no longer cut off in PDFs.
- Fixes for a looping formula, very deeply nested documents and a keystroke landing in the wrong file.
