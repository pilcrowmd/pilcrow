Two new ways to start writing, and three fixes.

- Create MD File: start a blank document straight from the welcome screen.
- A new setting opens documents in the editor instead of the reader.
- Pinch-to-zoom no longer leaves headings and paragraphs at different sizes.
- Right-to-left text in the editor now starts at the right edge.
- A document you start while your last file is still opening is no longer replaced.
