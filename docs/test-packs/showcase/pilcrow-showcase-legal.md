# Services Agreement: key clauses

This summary sets out the main terms between **Northfield Studio Ltd** (the **"Supplier"**) and **Brightmoor Supplies Ltd** (the **"Client"**). It is a sample for illustration and not legal advice.

## 1. Definitions

In this agreement:

1. **"Services"** means the design and development work described in the statement of work.
2. **"Deliverables"** means the files, documents and materials the Supplier produces for the Client.
3. **"Confidential Information"** means non-public information shared by either party, in any form.
4. **"Fees"** means the amounts set out in clause 4.

## 2. Services

1. The Supplier shall perform the Services with reasonable skill and care.
2. The Supplier shall:
   - assign a named project lead;
   - report progress every two weeks;
   - tell the Client promptly of any expected delay.
3. The Client shall provide:
   - timely access to staff and information;
   - written feedback within five working days of each delivery.

## 3. Term and termination

1. This agreement starts on the effective date and continues for twelve months.
2. Either party may end it:
   1. on thirty days' written notice; or
   2. immediately, if the other party commits a material breach and does not fix it within fourteen days of notice.
3. On termination the Client shall pay for all Services performed up to that date.[^1]

## 4. Fees and payment

1. The Client shall pay each invoice within thirty days of receipt.
2. Late amounts carry interest at a rate of 4% a year above the base rate.[^2]
3. Expenses need the Client's written approval in advance.

## 5. Confidentiality

Each party shall keep the other's **Confidential Information** secret and use it only for this agreement. This duty continues for three years after the agreement ends.

[^1]: Work in progress is valued at the daily rates in the statement of work, not at a share of the total fee.
[^2]: The base rate is the one published by the central bank on the due date of the unpaid invoice.
