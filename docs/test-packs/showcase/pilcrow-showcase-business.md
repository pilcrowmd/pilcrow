# Third quarter summary: Harbourlight Foods

Revenue grew **12.4%** on the same quarter last year, reaching **€4.82 million**. Margins held steady despite higher freight costs.

## Key figures

| Measure | Q3 | vs Q2 |
| ------- | -: | ----: |
| Revenue (€k) | 4,820 | +4.6% |
| Gross margin | 38.2% | +0.3 pt |
| Operating profit (€k) | 612 | +11.7% |
| Active customers | 3,140 | +5.2% |
| Orders per customer | 4.1 | +5.1% |

## Revenue by channel

| Channel | €k | Share |
| ------- | -: | ----: |
| Retail partners | 2,310 | 48% |
| Online shop | 1,560 | 32% |
| Restaurants | 950 | 20% |

## Highlights

- Online shop sales rose **21%**, helped by the new subscription box.
- Two regional retail chains added us to their winter ranges.
- Packaging changes cut waste by **9%** and lowered cost per unit.
- Freight costs rose **6%**, mostly on the northern routes.

## Risks and watch points

- One retail partner represents **18%** of revenue; renewal talks start in November.
- Oat and olive oil prices remain volatile.
- Warehouse capacity reaches its limit at about 5,500 orders a month.

> "We grew without cutting corners on quality. Customers are coming back, and that is the number we care about most."
>
> Head of Operations

## Outlook

Next quarter we expect revenue between **€5.1** and **€5.4 million**, with the winter ranges and holiday gift boxes as the main drivers. Capital spending of **€180,000** is planned for a second packing line.
